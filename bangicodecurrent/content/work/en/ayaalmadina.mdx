## The client

Ayaalmadina is a real estate developer and brokerage operating in the Tetouan region, selling new-build apartments and villas in urban and coastal developments. The team manages a portfolio of over 200 active listings across multiple project phases.

## The problem

The company's digital presence was a static brochure site with a contact form. Prospective buyers had no way to browse listings by criteria — they called the office or submitted a generic enquiry form, then waited for a salesperson to manually review availability, find relevant properties, and respond. The response lag averaged three days. The same property was often sent to multiple enquirers simultaneously, creating duplication and wasted sales effort.

## What we built

We built a property listing and enquiry platform on Laravel and MySQL, with Mapbox integration for an interactive map view. Buyers can filter by property type, number of bedrooms, price range, and proximity to landmarks — and see available units plotted on a live map. Each listing page shows floor plans, photos, key specs, and a direct enquiry form that routes to the responsible salesperson.

The admin panel lets the team publish a new listing in under a day, set it as available or reserved, and track which enquiries are associated with each unit. Enquiry routing ensures the right salesperson sees the lead immediately rather than it landing in a shared inbox.

## Outcome

Lead response time dropped from an average of three days to same-day acknowledgement. Duplicate enquiries — where the same buyer contacted the office multiple times because they hadn't heard back — fell by 80%. The team publishes new listings in hours rather than the week-long cycle that involved coordinating with an external web agency.
