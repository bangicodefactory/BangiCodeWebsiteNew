## The client

Alaturco is a Turkish and Mediterranean restaurant operating in northern Morocco. The brand serves a broad audience — families, professionals, tourists — with a menu spanning traditional mezze, grilled mains, and à la carte options. A strong in-person reputation hadn't been translated into a consistent social presence.

## The problem

The restaurant's social media was reactive rather than planned — posts went up when someone had a spare moment, and the content reflected that: over-lit phone shots, inconsistent formatting, and no through-line connecting the posts to a brand identity. The restaurant had real photogenic material — the food, the space, the kitchen — but no system for capturing and using it.

Follower counts were stagnant not because the audience wasn't there but because the content wasn't giving people a reason to engage or share.

## What we did

We built a 12-month content strategy anchored in photography and a defined visual system: consistent lighting approach, a fixed set of composition patterns, and a tone of voice for captions that matched the warmth of the in-person experience. We developed eight content formats — hero dish photography, kitchen moments, staff portraits, table setups, seasonal specials, testimonial graphics, story polls, and event announcements — giving the account variety without losing coherence.

Shoots happened monthly. Between shoots, we managed the posting calendar and engaged with comments and DMs on behalf of the restaurant. No paid promotion — the brief was to build a genuine community around the brand.

## Outcome

Monthly organic reach doubled within the first 90 days without any ad spend. The account developed a recognisable aesthetic that the restaurant team could point to as a reference when briefing other suppliers. The retainer ran for the full 12 months. By the end of the engagement, the restaurant's Instagram had become a meaningful source of table reservations — tracked via link-in-bio and direct message enquiries.
