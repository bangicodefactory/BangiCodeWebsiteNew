## The client

Riha.ma is a Moroccan lifestyle and home fragrance brand — candles, diffusers, and scented products rooted in Moroccan botanical traditions. The brand targets a young, urban Moroccan audience and sells primarily through its online store and physical stockists in major cities.

## The problem

The brand had a clear identity and strong product packaging, but its social content wasn't performing. Static product shots on Instagram were generating low engagement, and the brand had no presence on TikTok — where its target demographic was spending most of its time. Competitors with weaker products were outperforming Riha.ma purely on content volume and platform fluency.

The founder knew short-form video was the right format but didn't have the production resource or platform expertise to execute it consistently.

## What we did

We repositioned Riha.ma's social content around short-form video as the primary format, with TikTok and Instagram Reels as the distribution layer. Each month we produced 20+ short videos covering product showcases, scent story narratives, gifting content, behind-the-scenes manufacturing, and user-generated-style testimonials. Content was scripted for pacing and platform norms — TikTok hooks in the first two seconds, Reels optimised for audio-off viewing.

We also rebuilt the Instagram grid to function as a visual catalog: clean product photography as a reference layer, with Reels surfacing in the feed as discovery content. The two formats served different jobs — browse vs. engage — and we managed both.

## Outcome

TikTok views crossed 500K in the first three months of production. Website sessions attributable to social increased threefold, tracked via UTM parameters on link-in-bio. The brand gained a visible presence in the Moroccan lifestyle content space, with organic shares driving meaningful new-customer acquisition. The engagement retainer ran for 18 months.
