## The client

Nortecoffeeco is a specialty coffee roaster based in northern Morocco, sourcing single-origin beans and roasting in small batches. The brand sells directly to cafés and hospitality businesses as well as to individual customers who want fresh-roasted beans delivered to their door.

## The problem

The roaster had built a loyal wholesale customer base through word of mouth and phone orders, but the manual ordering process was becoming a bottleneck. Wholesale buyers — cafés and hotel purchasing managers — had to call or message to place orders, confirm pricing, and arrange delivery. There was no product catalogue, no way to track outstanding orders, and no way for the roaster to plan production based on incoming demand.

## What we built

We built two connected storefronts powered by Next.js and Sanity CMS: a consumer-facing retail site where individual buyers shop by origin, roast level, and grind setting, and a wholesale portal where registered B2B clients can browse the catalogue with their negotiated pricing, place standing orders, and track delivery status.

Sanity handles the full product catalogue — the roaster's team can update origins, tasting notes, stock status, and pricing tiers without touching code. Stripe powers both the retail checkout and the invoice-based wholesale flow.

## Outcome

Within three weeks of launch, the first wholesale orders came through the portal. Within two months, all wholesale ordering had moved online — zero phone-based orders. The roaster can now see aggregate demand before committing to a roasting schedule, reducing both waste and stockouts.
