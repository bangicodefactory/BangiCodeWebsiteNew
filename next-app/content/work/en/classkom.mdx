## The client

Classkom is a private education platform aimed at secondary and university-level students in Morocco. The founders wanted to build something closer to the way students actually learn — on their phones, at their own pace, with direct communication between students and instructors.

## The problem

Before Classkom, the instructors were running courses through a mix of WhatsApp groups, shared Google Drives, and informal payment transfers. There was no structured progression, no reliable way to track which students had completed what, and no separation between course content and personal communication. Students dropped off because they couldn't tell where they were in a course or what they were supposed to do next.

## What we built

We built Classkom as a mobile-first learning management system on React Native and Firebase, shipping simultaneously on iOS and Android. Students browse a course catalogue, enroll, and work through structured modules with video lessons, reading materials, and quizzes. Instructors have a dashboard to publish content, monitor student progress, and communicate via an in-app messaging system that keeps learning conversations separate from personal channels.

Firebase Realtime Database powers the live progress tracking — instructors can see which students are active, which are stuck, and which have completed each module. Payments are handled in-app with webhook-based enrollment confirmation.

## Outcome

Classkom onboarded over 500 active learners in its first semester of operation across 12 courses. The completion rate improved significantly compared to the previous informal arrangement because students always had a clear next step. Instructors reported spending less time on administration and more time responding to substantive questions.
