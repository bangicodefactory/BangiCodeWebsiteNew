## The client

Coinluminaire is a Moroccan retail brand selling decorative lighting and home accessories — pendant lights, wall fixtures, and ambient pieces sourced from artisan workshops across the region. The business started as a physical showroom and wanted to reach customers beyond its local market.

## The problem

The brand had an existing online presence, but it was static — a catalog of photos with no cart, no checkout, and no way to handle orders without phone calls. As the product range grew from a few dozen items to several hundred, maintaining even the static catalog became unmanageable. There was no stock tracking, no way to mark items as sold, and no visibility into which products were driving enquiry.

The owner needed a proper e-commerce storefront that could grow with the catalog and be managed without technical help.

## What we built

We built a full e-commerce platform on React, Node.js, and MongoDB. The storefront lets customers browse by category and room type, filter by price and style, view detailed product pages with multiple images, and check out directly on the site. The MongoDB document model was chosen deliberately — product attributes (materials, dimensions, bulb type, finish) vary significantly across categories, and a flexible schema avoids the brittle column sprawl that comes with a relational approach.

The admin panel is built for a non-technical operator: adding a product takes a form, a few image uploads, and a publish button. Stock status, pricing, and visibility are all editable without touching code. Order management is built in — incoming orders appear in a queue, and the owner can confirm, prepare, and mark as dispatched from the same interface.

## Outcome

The platform launched with three times the product count of the original static site. Page load on mobile came in under two seconds — important for the target audience browsing on mid-range phones. The owner now manages the full catalog solo, with no external agency involvement for routine updates. Phone-based order reconciliation dropped to zero.
