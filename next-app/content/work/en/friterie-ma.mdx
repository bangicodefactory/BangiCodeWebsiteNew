## The client

Friterie.ma is a fast-food chain expanding across northern Morocco, with locations in Tetouan, Martil, M'diq, and Fnideq. The brand is known for Belgian-style fries and burgers, and has a strong following among local students and families.

## The problem

The chain was growing faster than its ordering system could keep up. Each location took orders by phone or in person, with no shared visibility across sites. The owner couldn't track revenue by location without calling each manager, and there was no customer-facing online ordering at all. Staff spent time managing phone queues that could be automated.

## What we built

We built a custom e-commerce and ordering platform on React, Node.js, and MongoDB. Customers can browse the menu, customise their order, choose delivery or pickup, and pay via Stripe — all from their phone. Each location has its own management panel where staff see incoming orders in real time, mark them as ready, and handle the prep flow.

The admin dashboard gives the owner aggregated revenue, order counts, and bestseller data across all locations without any manual consolidation. We designed the schema to support easy addition of new locations — each with its own menu, hours, and pricing if needed.

## Outcome

Within three months of launch, Friterie.ma was processing over 1,200 orders per month through the platform. Phone orders dropped to near zero. The owner now reviews a weekly dashboard rather than calling each location for a sales update.
