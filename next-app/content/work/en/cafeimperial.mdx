## The client

Cafe Imperial is a café and restaurant in northern Morocco with a strong local following. The brand has a refined aesthetic — quality coffee, a curated food menu, and an atmosphere that draws a mix of regulars and visitors. The owners wanted a digital presence that matched their in-person experience.

## The problem

The business had no website. Customers found them through Instagram or word of mouth, with no single place to see the menu, opening hours, or location. When the menu changed seasonally, there was no way to communicate that online without coordinating with an external developer. The owners wanted control over their own content without needing to call an agency every time something changed.

## What we built

We built a clean, fast restaurant site on React and Node.js, designed around the café's visual identity — warm typography, considered whitespace, and high-quality food photography. The menu is structured as a data-driven section: the owners can edit a simple configuration file to update dishes, prices, and availability without touching code.

The site is bilingual — Arabic and French — with a language toggle that preserves the user's selection across sessions. Opening hours, location details, and a contact link are all surfaced at a glance on the home page, so customers don't need to hunt for basic information.

## Outcome

The site went live three weeks after the brief. The café team manages all content changes independently — seasonal menu updates, new items, and special hours are handled without agency involvement. The bilingual setup means the site serves both the local Arabic-speaking audience and French-speaking visitors without compromise.
