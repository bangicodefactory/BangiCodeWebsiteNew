## The client

Cosas Buenas is a curated lifestyle and gift retail brand with a warm, eclectic identity — carefully selected homeware, gifts, and lifestyle accessories sold through a physical boutique and an online shop. The name means "good things" in Spanish, and the brand leans into that idea: considered curation, quality over quantity, personal recommendations over algorithmic merchandising.

## The problem

The brand had an organic, personality-led Instagram presence that had built a modest but loyal following. The problem was consistency — posts were irregular, community responses were often delayed or missed, and there was no editorial calendar to build around product launches, seasonal moments, or promotions. The founder was managing social alongside everything else the business demanded, and it showed.

The brand's warmth and curation sensibility were genuinely differentiating — it just needed the infrastructure to express them reliably.

## What we did

We took on the full community and content layer for Cosas Buenas: editorial calendar, post production, caption writing, and community management. The content strategy centres on the brand's curation identity — every post is framed around _why_ something is worth owning, not just what it is. Photography uses the boutique's physical space as a backdrop, giving the feed a consistent location and warmth that studio shots couldn't replicate.

Community management is treated as a commercial channel: every DM and comment gets a response within 24 hours. For product enquiries, we route directly to the checkout link or flag for the founder's follow-up. No unanswered comment, no missed sale.

## Outcome

DM enquiries for products increased fivefold in the first 90 days. The comment response rate has been 100% since engagement began. The founder reports spending significantly less time on social while the account performs better — the content and community layer now runs without daily founder input. The retainer has been renewed each quarter since launch.
