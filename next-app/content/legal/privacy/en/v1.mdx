**Legal entity:** Bangicode SARL  
**Address:** Tetouan, Morocco  
**Contact:** legal@bangicode.ma

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and your rights under Moroccan Law 09-08 on the Protection of Personal Data and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

---

## Data we collect

### Contact form

When you submit the contact form on bangicode.ma we collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your company name (optional)
- Your message

We use this information to respond to your enquiry and, with your consent, to follow up about working together. We do not add you to a mailing list without explicit opt-in.

### Booking (Cal.com)

When you book a discovery call via our Cal.com integration we collect:

- Your name and email address
- Your preferred date and time
- Any notes you include in the booking form

This data is processed by Cal.com under their own privacy policy. We receive a copy of the booking details to manage our schedule.

### Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our website. GA4 collects:

- Pages visited and time spent
- Referring source (search engine, social platform, direct)
- General geographic region (country / city level — not precise location)
- Device and browser type

GA4 is loaded only after you accept analytics cookies via our cookie consent banner. No analytics data is collected before consent is given, and no data is collected at all if you decline.

### Session cookies

We set a first-party session cookie (`bgc_session`) to maintain basic site functionality. This cookie expires when you close your browser and contains no personally identifiable information.

### Cookie consent

We store your cookie consent preference in a first-party cookie (`bgc_consent_v1`). This cookie expires after 12 months and records whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies.

## Your rights

Under Moroccan Law 09-08 and, where applicable, GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **Access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion** — ask us to delete your data where there is no lawful reason to retain it
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdrawal of consent** — withdraw analytics consent at any time via the cookie banner

To exercise any of these rights, email **legal@bangicode.ma** with "Data request" in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days.

You may also file a complaint with the Moroccan data protection authority, the **Commission Nationale de Contrôle de la Protection des Données à Caractère Personnel (CNDP)**: [cndp.ma](https://www.cndp.ma).
