## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They serve different purposes — some are essential for a site to function, others remember your preferences, and some help website owners understand how visitors use their site.

---

## Cookies we use

### Essential cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function and cannot be switched off.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `bgc_session` | Maintains basic site functionality during your visit | Session (deleted when you close your browser) |
| `bgc_consent_v1` | Stores your cookie consent preference | 12 months |

### Analytics cookies (optional)

These cookies are only set if you accept analytics via the consent banner. They help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics 4 | Distinguishes unique users | 2 years |
| `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4 | Stores session state for GA4 | 1 year |

We do not use advertising, tracking, or social media cookies.

---

## Third-party cookies

The booking system (Cal.com) may set its own session cookies when you use the booking feature. These are governed by Cal.com's own cookie policy.

## Managing your preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time using the button below. Declining analytics cookies has no effect on site functionality — all features continue to work.

You can also control cookies directly through your browser settings. Note that blocking all cookies may affect how some pages display.
